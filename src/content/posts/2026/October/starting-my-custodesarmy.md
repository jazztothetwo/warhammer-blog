This is going to be a long one.

## Why Custodes and why now?

Custodes have always really interested me. An army of the Emperor's greatest, small model count, useable in both 40k and Heresy. The Custodes are the pinnacle of humanity. Standing above all, even the Astartes. A single Custodian is themself a near unstoppable army. They are only bested by the Emperor himself. What's not to love?

The models.

I didn't love the models.

The Custodes, as a playable army in both 40k and 30k, only came out in mid 2016. All things considered, they are a relatively recent faction and as such, one would expect their models to look fairly decent. And I'm sure at the time, pre Primaris, pre scale growth, they did.

I, however, started Warhammer during the Era Indomitus. My first introduction to Warhammer was with Primaris Space Marines. Once I learnt about Custodes, I still thought they were cool. But comparing them to Primaris? They looked, in my opinion, bad. They were squatty. They stood a head shorter than Primaris. There was a disconnect there. A disconnect between the Custodes of the lore and the models on the table. The models didn't make me excited. And as such, I continued on my Warhammer journey and left them behind.

To be clear, i still **loved** the Custodes whenever they would appear in art, stories, and animations. Episode 2 of The Tithes, about a Custodian and a Sister of Silence overseeing a Black Ship and collecting a tithe of human psykers, got me so incredibly hyped. But after watching that animation, I went to the Warhammer web store and... was immediately reminded why I wasn't already collecting Custodes.

Then, one fateful January morning in 2026, the New Year Preview dropped. And what did i see? But brand new Custodes of course. Suddenly the models matched what I'd always imagined Custodes should look like. But we weren't just greeted by new plastic Custodian Guard and Custodian Sentinels. There were brand new **plastic** Caladius Annihilator Grav-tanks. **Plastic** Contemptor-Achillus and Contemptor-Galatus. New and **plastic** EVERYTHING.
